What your dataset contains

Both CSV files include company name, company rating and a text field with industry, employee range, ownership/recognition, company age and city information. The chart recommendations above use these fields.

!

Before building the charts

Extract industry, employee size and company age from other_data. Remove the Unnamed: 0 index column, check duplicate companies, and handle missing ratings. These CSVs list companies, not individual job vacancies, so avoid labeling the row count as “jobs available.”
